Our Story

UU FakeGym was founded by a group of Ulster University students in 2026 with a simple belief: getting fit should not cost a fortune.

We wanted to create an affordable, welcoming gym for students and the wider Belfast community - without expensive memberships or unnecessary contract stress.

Today, we are proud to be Belfasts most affordable gym, offering 24/7 access and a place where everyone can feel comfortable working towards their goals.

Our Mission

Making fitness affordable, accessible, and welcoming for every student — because everyone deserves a place to get stronger.
